# 🔎 Weekly Retrospectives

This section contains my weekly study reviews and reflections.

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal is to evaluate my progress, identify knowledge gaps, and continuously improve my learning routine.

## 📝 Weekly Review Template

For each week, I will document:

### 1. What did I study?
Summarize the topics covered during the week.

### 2. What did I build?
List the exercises, features, and projects I completed.

### 3. What did I learn?
Explain the most important concepts in my own words.

### 4. What was difficult?
Describe the problems, mistakes, or concepts I struggled with.

### 5. How did I solve those problems?
Record the debugging process, solutions, and useful references.

### 6. What do I need to review?
List concepts that are still unclear.

### 7. What are my next steps?
Define the main learning goals for the following week.

## 📈 Progress Indicators

- Consistency of study
- Practical exercises completed
- Ability to explain concepts
- Ability to solve problems independently
- Code quality improvements

## 💡 Guideline

Progress is not measured only by the number of topics completed, but also by my ability to apply what I have learned.
